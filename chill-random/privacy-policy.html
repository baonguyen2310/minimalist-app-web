Privacy Policy for Chill Random

Last Updated: April 2025

Introduction

Thank you for choosing Chill Random, a simple random generator app. This Privacy Policy is designed to help you understand how we handle your information.

Information We Don't Collect

Chill Random is designed with your privacy in mind. We're proud to confirm that our app:

  • Does not collect personal information
  • Does not request any permissions

  • Does not share any data with third parties

How We Operate

Chill Random operates entirely on your device. All random generations are processed locally, meaning your data never leaves your device.

Children's Privacy

Chill Random does not collect information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.
